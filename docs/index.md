# Hearth & Herald

Turn your Bannerlord clan into a real institution: build your retinue from the
field, seat a Clan Home, staff five herald offices, and run recruitment,
careers, family, and intrigue through your own household.

[Get the mod on Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/11742)

Use the version picker in the header to match this documentation to the mod
version you are playing. `latest` always tracks the newest release.

## The two pillars

**Heralds** - the governance layer. Once your clan owns a town or castle, it
becomes your Clan Home, a Master Herald takes up residence, and you can appoint
specialists to four offices (Exchequer, Watch, Muster, Arms) that deliver
clan-wide bonuses, warnings, and actions.

**Households** - the people layer. Ennoble proven veterans into companions,
grant them commissions (Marshal, Castellan, Caravan Master), recruit commoners
and converted prisoners, and manage romance, marriage, mentoring, and heirs.
